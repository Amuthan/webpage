Hello, welcome to my academic webpage! 

I'm an Assistant Professor (tenure-track) in the [Aerospace Engineering](https://aero.calpoly.edu) department at [Cal Poly](https://www.calpoly.edu) since Fall 2024. I broadly work in the areas of theoretical and computational mechanics, multiscale modeling, and scientific computing. At Cal Poly, I teach courses related to aerospace structural analysis.

Prior to joining Cal Poly, I was an Assistant Professor of [Aerospace Engineering](https://www.aero.iitb.ac.in/) at [IIT Bombay](https://www.iitb.ac.in/). I obtained my B.Tech./M.Tech. dual degree in [Aerospace Engineering](https://ae.iitm.ac.in/) from [IIT Madras](https://www.iitm.ac.in/) in 2006, and subsequently obtained my Ph.D. in [Aerospace Engineering](https://aero.iisc.ac.in/) from [IISc Bangalore](https://www.iisc.ac.in) in 2012. I was a postdoctoral researcher at [Ecole Centrale de Nantes](https://www.ec-nantes.fr/english-version) in 2013, and at [Caltech](https://www.caltech.edu/) from 2013 to 2017.
